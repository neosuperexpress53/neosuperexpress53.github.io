Once Upon Online

Friendly Neighbourhood Bookstore!

This is a bookstore that puts customers on the forefront of our business. Founded in 2018, Once Upon Online was created for book lovers, by book lovers. We wanted a place for people to get their favourite books to enjoy, from all over the nation. We want to put your best reads right into your hands. Once Upon Online will fulfill your literary needs!

This website is managed by Calvin Nguyen and Yanyu Chen, two coders that love reading books of all kinds! We worked tirelessly and relentlessly to bring you this humble little bookstore. Our goal is to share our love of literature to all whom are interested. We appreciate any and all customers, whether you window shop or pick up a few books for yourself. Please come and pay us a visit. Stick around for a while, will ya?
